Title:
Zero-Naira Declutter Hustle: Start a Resale Agency With Just Your Phone

Description:
Learn how to build a paying declutter/resale agency business in Nigeria — no capital, no stock, no shop. This guide shows you how to connect sellers and buyers of used household items via WhatsApp, earn commission on every match, and use free AI tools (Claude, Gemini, Canva) to price items, write listings, and enhance photos. Includes the 4-Step Sale Cycle, a Buyer Request Board system, commission pricing models, and a 30-day launch plan to get your first sale.
